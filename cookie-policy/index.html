Quick summary

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising tags. Fonts are served from our own domain, so loading a page doesn't contact third-party font or tracking services.

01

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser, for example to keep you signed in or to measure visits.

02

What this site uses

  • No cookies of any kind, essential or otherwise
  • No analytics or advertising tools
  • No local storage used to track you

Because nothing is set, there's no cookie banner to accept.

03

Other websites

When you follow a link to a client's site, an email provider or a map, that site may set its own cookies under its own policy.

04

Your browser controls

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. It won't affect how this site works.

05

If this changes

If we add analytics or a booking tool that uses cookies, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where required before any non-essential cookie is set.
