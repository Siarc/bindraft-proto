1. Who We Are
BindRaft ("we," "our," or "us") provides customized digital solutions and AI-powered agent services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your personal information.
This Privacy Policy applies when you:
Visit our website
Use our services or AI solutions
Contact our team or create an account
By interacting with us, you agree to the practices described here. If you disagree, please discontinue use of our services.

4. AI Services & Data Processing
Because our core offerings involve AI agent services, we want to be clear about how your data is handled in this context:
Service Delivery
Interaction data processed through our AI agents is used solely to deliver, refine, and maintain the functionality of the service you requested.
We do not use your personal conversations to identify you or target you with advertising.
Model Improvement & Anonymization
When we use operational data to improve our AI models, we first remove all Personally Identifiable Information (PII) through technical anonymization processes.
Only aggregated, non-identifiable datasets are used for model training.
We do not train our models on raw, identifiable user data.
Third-Party AI Infrastructure
We may use secure, vetted third-party providers (e.g., cloud platforms or foundational model APIs) to power our services. All such partners are bound by strict Data Processing Agreements that:
Prohibit them from using your data to train their own commercial models
Require confidentiality and security safeguards aligned with our standards
Limit data use to the specific purposes we authorize
5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies
What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit. We use:
Essential cookies:
Required for site functionality and security
Analytics cookies:
Help us understand how visitors use our site (e.g., via Google Analytics)
Preference cookies:
Remember your settings and choices
Managing Your Preferences
You can control cookie settings through your browser or device preferences.
Disabling non-essential cookies may limit some site features but will not affect core service delivery.

BindRaft operates globally. When we transfer your personal data outside your country of residence (including to countries that may not have equivalent privacy laws), we implement appropriate safeguards such as:

8. How Long We Keep Your Data
We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to:
Provide and maintain our services
Comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations
Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements
Retention Guidelines:
Account and contact information:
Retained while your account is active, plus up to 3 years after last activity for security and compliance purposes
Usage and analytics data:
Aggregated or anonymized after 24 months; raw logs retained for up to 12 months
AI interaction data:
Anonymized for model improvement within 90 days; identifiable data deleted after service completion unless retention is required for support or legal reasons
Specific retention periods may vary based on jurisdictional requirements. If you request deletion, we will securely erase your data unless an exception applies (e.g., legal hold, fraud prevention).
