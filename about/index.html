Home / About

Bind together to stay afloat.

It's an old line of ours and still how we work: small team, close to the client, hands on the code.

01 / Origin

BindRaft is a software studio in Uttara, Dhaka, working since 2023. We work as a product-engineering partner: we design and build the software, then stay on to run it with you if you want that.

Our clients range from courier brands to multi-company groups to export manufacturers. They hire us when the website or the tool has to work in daily use, not just look good in a pitch.

02 / How we work

Five working rules

  • 01Show, don't tell

    Clickable designs and staging links over slide decks and status reports.

  • 02Boring where it counts

    Proven stacks, readable code, docs you can hand to the next developer.

  • 03Honest scope

    We say what we won't build, and why, before we quote.

  • 04You own it

    Repos, accounts and credentials are in your name from day one.

  • 05Close to the work

    Direct contact with the people building it, not a chain of account managers.
