Quick summary

This site has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. If you email us or send a request through the contact page, we get what you write and use it to reply. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs. That's it.

01

Who we are

BindRaft ("we", "us") is a software studio at Road-10, House-31, Sector-9, Uttara, Dhaka 1230, Bangladesh. This policy covers this website only. Work we do for clients is covered by the agreement we sign with each client.

02

What we collect

When you contact us

The contact form and the intro-call picker don't submit anything to our servers. They open your own email app with a message addressed to us. When you send it, we receive:

  • Your name and email address
  • Anything you choose to write, such as your company, project details or a preferred call time

The same applies if you email or call us directly.

When you browse

Like any website host, our hosting provider records basic request data (IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time) to run and secure the service.

What we don't collect

  • No user accounts or passwords
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking pixels
  • No payment details
03

How we use it

  • To reply to you and talk about your project
  • To prepare a proposal or estimate if you ask for one
  • To keep the site secure and working

We don't sell your data and we don't add you to a mailing list.

04

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the site and answer you: our email provider and our website hosting provider. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

05

Where it's processed

Our email and hosting providers may store or process data outside Bangladesh. We pick established providers that protect data in transit and at rest.

06

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as the conversation, and any project that follows from it, needs them, plus any period the law requires for business records. Server logs are kept by the hosting provider for a limited period under its own retention settings.

07

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the messages and contact details we hold about you. Email contact@bindraft.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

08

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to our inbox is limited to the people who handle enquiries.

09

Children

This site is meant for businesses and isn't directed at children.

10

Changes to this policy

If what the site collects changes, for example if we add analytics or an online booking tool, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.
