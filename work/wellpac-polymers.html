01 / Problem

Overseas buyers check a supplier's website before they ask for a quote.

Wellpac makes HDPE, LDPE and LLDPE flexible packaging for export to the UK, US, Japan and Europe. Its site needs to show a procurement manager what the plant can make and how to get in touch.

02 / Constraints
  • 01The audience is international B2B buyers, not local consumers
  • 02The product range has to be easy to scan, so it's built around a gallery
  • 03Plant capability (their stated 60,000 sq ft, 8-colour printing) shown without overselling
  • 04Needs to fit within the wider Silkways group presence
03 / What we built

An industrial export-manufacturer site with a product gallery.

A focused manufacturer site with a product gallery for the packaging range, plant capability, and enquiry routes for overseas buyers.
